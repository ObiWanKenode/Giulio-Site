Privacy Policy – Stellar Block

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Versione italiana

Stellar Block is an Android game developed by Culobomb Studio. This page explains what data is processed when you play.

Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, the IP address (from which it derives an approximate location), information about the device and about your interaction with the ads. This data is processed by Google according to its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game it asks for your consent through Google's platform. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

If you buy Premium you no longer see ads.

In-app purchases

Themes and Premium are bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google: the game neither receives nor stores your payment details, only confirmation of which items you have bought.

Data saved on your device

The game saves only on your device your high scores (for each mode and difficulty), your progress on the Star Hunt map, your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, piece ghost, language, last difficulty chosen) and the themes you selected. This data is not sent to any of the developer's servers and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Bug reports and suggestions

From Report a bug (in Settings and in the pause menu) you can write us a title and a description and attach up to 3 images. The game does not send anything by itself: it prepares an email that is sent from your own email app, only if you choose to press Send. In that case we receive your email address, the text and images you chose, and some technical information added to the message (device model, Android version, game version, screen size, game language and the mode you were playing). We use it only to fix the problem or evaluate the suggestion and to reply to you; we do not share it with anyone and we delete it when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it by writing to the address below. The selected images are temporarily copied into a private folder of the game, which is emptied at the next report or when you uninstall the game.

Account and personal data

The game does not require you to create an account. Apart from the reports you decide to send us by email, the developer does not collect your name, email address or any other personal data.

Permissions

The game uses Vibration for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings) and Internet access for ads and purchases.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with the new date.
